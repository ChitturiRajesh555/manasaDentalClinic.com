How is an Orthodontist different from a dentist:

While both professionals are dedicated to taking care of your oral health, there is a subtle difference in the kind of specialization they undergo. While dentists specialize in complete dental care that includes cleaning, fillings, teeth whitening, and crowns, orthodontists specialize in preventing and treating misaligned teeth. To achieve that, orthodontists use braces, palatal expanders, Invisalign, and other tools.

When to See an Orthodontist?

With the knowledge of what oral care you need, you have to decide if you would require a dentist or an orthodontist. Here are some of the most common reasons people will go to an orthodontist:

If you have teeth that are crowded or overlapping, then an orthodontist can help you straighten them and put a big smile on your face.

Many people have large gaps and uneven spaces between their teeth, which can be a simple fix through Invisalign.

If there is pain in your jaws or if you feel there is a misalignment in your jaws, then an orthodontist can bring relief to that pain through the right treatment.

You can see an orthodontist, in case there is an improper alignment of teeth when you close your mouth.

There are other issues that are seen in babies like early or late loss of baby teeth, breathing through the mouth, and thumb-sucking can require an orthodontist’s attention.

What is Invisalign?

To understand that, we have to know what Aligners are. Aligners are basically 3D digitally scanned aligners which are created to treat misaligned teeth. Invisalign is a brand of clear aligners manufactured by the US brand, Align Technology. So far, more than 14 million patients have benefited through Invisalign.

In India, Invisalign entered the market just a few years ago and has already taken the nation by storm. It negates all the disadvantages of regular braces and makes the whole process of aligning misaligned teeth better, and comfortable.

 Benefits of Invisalign:

  • As the name suggests, the aligners are invisible, which makes it a discreet option for people to straighten their teeth.
  • Unlike braces, which are made of metal, these are made of plastic, so they are automatically way more comfortable than the usual braces.
  • They can be easily removed while brushing, eating, or flossing, which is a huge inconvenience in traditional braces.
  • Because it is 3D printed, patients can gauge and predict results before starting their treatment.
  • It is customized to the last inch and fits perfectly to the exact shape of your teeth.

 How long does Invisalign treatment typically take?

The duration of Invisalign treatment varies depending on the complexity of the case but generally ranges from 12 to 18 months. However, some patients may see results in as few as 6 months.

 Is Invisalign treatment painful?

Invisalign aligners may cause mild discomfort when a new aligner is first used, but this typically subsides within a few days. Most patients find Invisalign more comfortable than traditional braces.

 Can anyone get Invisalign treatment?

Invisalign is suitable for most teens and adults who have mild to moderate orthodontic issues. However, a consultation with an Invisalign-certified orthodontist is necessary to determine if it’s the right option for your specific needs.

 How often do I need to visit the orthodontist during Invisalign treatment?

Patients typically visit their orthodontist every 4 to 6 weeks to ensure the treatment is progressing as planned and to receive their next set of aligners. However, this can vary based on individual treatment plans.

What is Invisalign and how does it work?

Invisalign is a modern orthodontic treatment that uses clear, plastic aligners to gradually straighten teeth without the need for traditional metal braces. These custom-made aligners are virtually invisible and are replaced every two weeks to move teeth into the desired position over time.

How do I choose the best Invisalign orthodontist in Chennai?

When looking for the best Invisalign orthodontist in Chennai, consider factors such as the orthodontist’s experience with Invisalign treatments, patient reviews, before-and-after photos of previous patients, professional qualifications, and the technology and facilities available at their clinic.

What can I expect during my initial consultation with an Invisalign orthodontist?

During your initial consultation, the orthodontist will assess your dental structure, discuss your orthodontic goals, and determine if you are a good candidate for Invisalign. They may also use digital imaging technology to create a 3D model of your teeth and show you a virtual preview of your potential post-treatment smile.

How long does Invisalign treatment typically take in Chennai?

The duration of Invisalign treatment varies depending on the complexity of the case, but on average, it can range from 12 to 18 months. Your orthodontist in Chennai will provide you with a more accurate timeline based on your specific dental needs.

What is the approximate cost of Invisalign treatment in Chennai?
